A Home-Food Revolution by Home Cooks

Our journey sprouted in 2020 and has since bloomed into a network of super chefs, building a better future while cooking from home.

We are the world’s first and largest home food platform created by home cooks. Powered by thousands of women making delicious , healthy foods for the world.

The 4 Pillars Of Our Core Philosophy

Humility

At Shero, we foster an environment where every voice is valued and every contribution is celebrated. It's in acknowledging each other's strengths and weaknesses that we truly flourish as a team.

Transparency

We believe in open communication, honesty, and accountability. By sharing information openly and candidly, we cultivate a culture of trust, where everyone feels empowered and informed.

Dedication

Whether it's serving our customers with excellence or supporting our home cooks with unwavering loyalty, dedication is the driving force behind our collective success.

Integrity

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of integrity, always choosing to do what is right. It's in upholding our values and principles that we earn the trust and respect of our customers and communities.
